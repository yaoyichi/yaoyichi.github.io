UMN DATA SCIENCE M.S. WEBSITE DRAFT

Open index.html in Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge.

No local server, installation, or sign-in is required. The page layout,
stylesheet, and display image are stored in this folder. Links for admissions,
curriculum, email, and University resources still open their official online
destinations and therefore require an internet connection.

This is a planning draft, not an official University of Minnesota webpage.

Maroon dashed cards labeled "Content not ready" are
intentional placeholders. They identify content that still requires current,
program-specific evidence or an approved asset before the page is published.
